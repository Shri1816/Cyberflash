About CyberFlash

The fastest way to master cybersecurity fundamentals

🎯

What is CyberFlash?

CyberFlash is an interactive flashcard-based learning platform designed to help you rapidly absorb cybersecurity knowledge through spaced repetition, gamification, and active recall.

👥

Who is it for?

Built for cybersecurity students, aspiring SOC analysts, ethical hackers preparing for certifications (CEH, OSCP, CompTIA Security+), and IT professionals expanding their security skills.

📚

What will you learn?

Master 4 core domains: Computer Networks, Linux OS, Security Operations Center workflows, and Ethical Hacking — from fundamentals to advanced professional-level concepts.

🚀

Why flashcards?

Active recall through flashcards is proven to be more effective than passive reading. The gamified Rapid Fire mode adds competitive urgency that supercharges retention.
